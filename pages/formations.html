Turn troop inventory + march capacity into per-formation recipes the in-game UI can actually dial — absolute counts, Balance % of your real capacity (never the join cap), and whether to Save as Count or Save as Ratio.
Army & marches
How to use in-game
  1. Open a formation slot.
  2. Enter the Balance % shown (these are % of your real march capacity).
  3. Confirm the absolute troop counts match the recipe.
  4. Save as Count or Ratio exactly as recommended.
Whale hunts often set a join cap (e.g. 100k) so more players fit. That cap limits troops sent — it is not the denominator for Balance %.
